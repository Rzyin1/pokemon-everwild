### Custom Type Effectiveness

| Attacking Type | 2× Damage Against | 0.5× Damage Against |
|---|---|---|
| Sound | Water, Flying, Psychic | Rock, Ground, Sound |
| Light | Dark, Ghost, Poison | Fire, Grass, Steel, Light |
| Mist | Fire, Ground, Psychic | Electric, Grass, Ice, Mist |

| Defending Type | Takes 2× From | Takes 0.5× From |
|---|---|---|
| Sound | Electric, Rock | Water, Flying, Sound |
| Light | Dark, Rock, Steel | Ghost, Fairy, Light |
| Mist | Electric, Flying, Ice | Fire, Water, Ground, Mist |

All other interactions involving Sound, Light, and Mist are neutral. The three new types introduce no immunities.

## Core Gameplay Loop

1. Roll 1d20 to determine which starter monster chooses the player.
2. Establish the initial monster bond.
3. Prepare for an expedition by purchasing supplies and breathing capsules.
4. Pay the entry fee to enter the Rift.
5. Arrive at a random location within the Rift.
6. Explore environments and experience zone-specific random encounters.
7. Battle dangerous monsters and potentially bond with eligible first-stage monsters.
8. Manage the remaining encounter-based breathing capacity.
9. Use one-time capsules to extend the expedition when needed.
10. Automatically return to the human world when breathing capacity reaches zero.
11. Complete quests and progress through the class rank system.
12. Prepare for future expeditions.

**Design status:** Working master reference. Confirmed decisions remain established unless explicitly revised; pending systems are open for development.
