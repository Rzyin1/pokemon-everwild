## Approval process
1. Choose a concept and basic appearance.
2. Agree on typing, name, and evolution plan.
3. Develop mechanics and Pokédex lore.
4. Approve the species before assigning its permanent number and adding it to `data/pokedex.json`.

Artwork and sprites should be original. Do not add copyrighted assets from official games.
