Ideas Board

A place to keep our concepts, approved decisions, and questions for later.

Approved concept

Starter selection system

  • 18 original starter lines: one for each Pokémon type.
  • Each starter line has three evolutionary stages, for 54 starter Pokémon total.
  • At the professor's lab, three different-type starters are randomly offered.
  • The player examines the three choices and picks one.
To decide

Rival selection, reroll rules, and whether other starters become obtainable later.

Region and Gyms · Approved

Large region with more than eight Gyms

Approved

Pokémon Everwild is a traditional Pokémon-style RPG set in a large explorable region, with more than eight Gyms and a substantial map containing towns, cities, routes, caves, and other exploration areas. The exact number of Gyms, map layout, Gym challenge order, and League qualification requirements will be decided later; do not assume an eight-Gym limit.
